# Stashbox: platform tactics

Stashbox-specific guidance per destination. Loaded by section, on top of
`shared/platforms.md`. Cadence numbers and promo caps are in `policy.json`
and enforced by code.

## [instagram] Instagram

Sector × format, from Dean's Instagram ecosystem sheet:

| Sector | Reel | Carousel | Image post | Story |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| live-shows | Live show clip | Tour/show highlights | Show promo | "Get tickets / book us" |
| radio | Song/radio promo | Featured songs | "On Air" show graphic | Live now / listen |
| radio-blog | Studio/platform walkthrough | Client use cases | Service spotlight | Custom solutions |
| merch | Merch reel / new drop | Merch collection | Hoodie/tee drop | New merch |
| streaming | New release | Listen everywhere | Album spotlight | Song preview |
| custom-production | Studio session | Client use cases | "For your brand" | Custom solutions |
| youtube tie-in | Music video clip | Behind the scenes | Subscribe | — |

- Reels are the main reach engine: performance clips first.
- Stories carry the direct links (link sticker): song links, product links, booking.

## [tiktok] TikTok

- Performance clips are the core: start on the downbeat, a chorus or a crowd moment.
- 40% of slots are exploration (config): test hooks, clip lengths, songs and
  sectors that haven't run here yet.
- Business sectors (radio-blog, custom-production, elettro) only as exploration tests.

## [youtube] YouTube (@Stashboxband)

- Shorts: daily performance clips; title names the show and song, e.g.
  "Stashbox Does Dylan: Simple Twist of Fate (live)".
- Long-form: full-song videos and full sets. Shorts point to the full video when one exists.
- CTA: the post's own CTA, then always close the description with the
  subscribe reminder to @stashboxband (goal #1); song links in description.

## [facebook] Facebook

- Strongest platform for South Florida bookings: lead live-shows posts with
  place and event context (from records), and invite venues/planners to message.
- Share show clips with a booking line; events from show records.

## [threads] Threads

- Conversational band voice: behind-the-scenes thoughts, song stories,
  questions to fans. Promotional posts stay within the 25% cap (config).
- Replies are suggested, not sent.

## [x] X

- Short takes, song links, show clips. Promotional posts within the 25% cap (config).
- Replies are suggested, not sent.

## [linkedin] LinkedIn

- Business development only: radio-blog, custom-production and elettro
  sectors. The angle is "here's what we built and what it does for a business."

## [pinterest] Pinterest

- Merch (product pins with product links) and song artwork (pins to the song's
  radio link) are the natural fit; evergreen performance stills to YouTube.

## [gmb] Google Business Profile

- Live-shows and custom-production for South Florida searchers: updates with
  a **Book** or **Learn more** button to the booking/contact record.

## [reddit] Reddit

- Handoff only. Performance clips and song stories shared as the band, in
  communities that welcome them. Never merch or business sectors.
