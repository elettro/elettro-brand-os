# Stashbox

Brand intelligence for Stashbox. The core (everything before the first
bracketed section) is loaded on every Stashbox decision. Sector sections load
when a slot is assigned that sector.

Source: Dean Palermo's decisions of 2026-10-17 and the Stashbox ecosystem
sheets. Numbers (shares, cooldowns, cadence) live in `policy.json`.

## Who Stashbox is

Stashbox is a South Florida band and music company. **Music · Shows · Radio ·
Merch · Custom.**

- **The band** performs two booked live shows: **Stashbox Does Dylan** (Bob
  Dylan tribute) and **Stashbox Does Sublime & Reggae**. Both are available for
  booking in South Florida.
- **Stashbox Radio** (`stashbox.com/radio`) is Stashbox's own streaming
  platform for original music. Anyone can play full songs; free registration
  unlocks dynamic videos, personal share tracking and playlists.
- **stashbox.ai** is the official merch store (about 600 products).
- **Streaming:** Stashbox releases are on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music
  and TIDAL.
- **Custom music and video production** for businesses, brands and events,
  made in house with bleeding-edge production tools.
- Stashbox's social presence is built and run by **Elettro**, and doubles as a
  live demonstration of what Elettro builds for clients.

## Audience

- **Fans:** reggae, reggae-rock, Sublime and Dylan listeners; live-music
  lovers; South Florida locals and visitors.
- **Bookers:** venues, bars, resorts, private and corporate event planners in
  South Florida.
- **Business buyers:** bands, labels, resorts and businesses who want a music
  platform, custom music or a social system like Stashbox's.

## What matters in a Stashbox asset

When analyzing Stashbox media, these four facts matter most. Find them every
time, with evidence:

1. **Song**: which song is being played (radio, streaming and the 1-day
   same-song rule all depend on it).
2. **Session / date**: which session or show it came from (e.g. "Studio One
   2026-10-11"), plus the capture date. New-batch boosts and session grouping
   depend on it.
3. **Location**: Stashbox's studio or the concert venue, from the session
   record, GPS or visible signage.
4. **Who's in it**: band members and guests by role from the session roster
   (vocals, guitar, bass, drums, keys). Name them only through the roster.

Also note which show it is (Does Dylan or Does Sublime & Reggae) and crowd
presence: a live crowd is a strong signal for the live-shows sector.

## Voice

*Interim, from Dean's approved materials. Replace with Dean's voice notes when
provided.*

- Real music, real experiences: grounded in actual performances, songs and
  products. Never hype that the footage doesn't back up.
- Warm, confident, musician-to-listener. A band talking, not a corporation.
- Specific over superlative: name the song, the moment, the place.
- Fan sectors sound like the band; business sectors sound like a capable
  production partner. Same honesty in both.

## Restrictions

- **Covers and tributes are honest.** Dylan and Sublime material is "Stashbox
  Does Dylan / Sublime": the band's performance of those songs. Never imply
  Bob Dylan, Sublime or their estates perform, endorse or are affiliated.
- **Bookings are South Florida.** Don't offer bookings elsewhere unless a show
  record says so.
- **Show dates, venues and ticket links only from show records.**
- **Merch prices and availability only from live Shopify data** at the time of
  the post.
- **Radio posts link to the song's own radio link** (`stashbox.com/radio/?song=<key>`
  from the song record), not the radio homepage, unless the post is about the
  platform itself.
- **Cross-brand promotion:** Stashbox may promote **Elettro** (sector
  `elettro`, and as a closing line on `radio-blog` and `custom-production`
  posts). No other cross-brand links.
- **People:** name band members and guests only when the asset's linked
  subjects name them.

## [live-shows] Live shows: Stashbox Does Dylan, Stashbox Does Sublime & Reggae

- **Goal:** get booked. Every post should leave a venue or event planner
  thinking "this would work at our place."
- **Show:** the band at full energy: crowd reactions, singalongs, solos, the
  room filling up, the band locked in. Both shows, kept in balance unless a
  campaign favors one.
- **Angles:** the moment (this chorus, this solo), the crowd, the venue
  atmosphere, the musicianship, "what your event could feel like."
- **CTA:** book the show for your venue or event in South Florida. Link/contact
  from the booking record (booking page or `booking@stashbox.com`).
- **Fan secondary CTA (only when a show record exists):** come see us, with
  date/venue from the record.
- **Name the show** in title or first line ("Stashbox Does Dylan", "Stashbox
  Does Sublime & Reggae"), and the song when known.
- **Success:** booking inquiries, then saves/shares/views.

## [radio] Stashbox Radio

- **Goal:** a listen first, then a free registration.
- **Show:** one song per post: artwork, a clip, the dynamic video, the story
  behind the song. Rotate across the catalog; feature different songs.
- **CTA pattern:** **Listen now** (the song's radio link, plays instantly, no
  login) → **Join free** for the dynamic videos, your own playlists and
  tracking your shares. Rotate which member benefit leads.
- **Teasers:** a short look at a members' dynamic video is a strong hook
  ("the full video's inside, free to join").
- **Success:** registrations attributed to the post, then plays.

## [radio-blog] Radio blog: we build this for you

- **Goal:** business leads from bands, labels, resorts and businesses who want
  a music platform and ecosystem like Stashbox Radio.
- **Show:** the ecosystem working: the radio player, dynamic videos, share
  tracking, playlists, the publishing system behind it, real results.
- **Source:** posts on `stashbox.com/radio/blog`. Each social post teases one
  blog post and links to it.
- **Voice:** capable production partner. Outcomes and how it works, not fan-speak.
- **CTA:** read the post / talk to us about building yours. Optional closing
  line to Elettro.
- **Success:** blog visits from business audiences, inquiries.

## [merch] stashbox.ai merch

- **Goal:** product sales.
- **Source:** the stashbox.ai Shopify catalog: titles, descriptions, images,
  prices, collections.
- **Keep it fresh:** about 600 products means no product should feel repeated.
  Rotate products, collections and angles: new drop, collection spotlight,
  design close-up, lifestyle/wearing it, gift idea, tie-in to a song (via
  song-specific products when the record links them).
- **Facts:** product names, prices and availability from live data only.
- **CTA:** shop the product (product URL from the record).
- **Success:** product clicks and orders.

## [streaming] Streaming: Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, TIDAL

- **Goal:** streams and Spotify follows.
- **Show:** the song's official artwork, a clip, release info from the song record.
- **CTA:** listen on your platform (song-specific streaming links from the
  record) and follow Stashbox on Spotify (artist profile link). Use only links
  present on the record.
- **Releases:** new releases and catalog features; licensed covers presented honestly.
- **Success:** streaming link clicks, Spotify follows.

## [custom-production] Custom music and video production

- **Goal:** clients for custom music and video production.
- **Show:** the work itself as proof: Stashbox's own music videos, edits,
  visual effects, promos, custom radio solutions. Before/after and process
  clips work well.
- **Audience:** businesses, brands, events, artists.
- **CTA:** talk to us about your project (contact link from record). Optional
  closing line to Elettro.
- **Success:** inquiries.

## [elettro] Like how Stashbox posts? (Elettro)

- **Goal:** clients for Elettro's social media systems.
- **Message:** everything you see on Stashbox's channels is produced and
  published by the system Elettro builds. "Like how Stashbox posts? Elettro can
  set this up for you."
- **Show:** the feed itself, the variety across platforms, behind-the-scenes of
  the system.
- **CTA:** contact Elettro at elettro.com (contact link from record).
- **Tone:** confident, brief, never disparaging other approaches.
