## Voice

**Innovative, hopeful and passionate about setting up world-class digital brand systems** (Dean, 2026-11-07).

- Innovative: lead with what is new or smart about the work: the system, the automation, the idea behind it.
- Hopeful: optimistic about what a business can become with the right system; never cynical, never fear-based.
- Passionate: write like the people who build it and love it, specific and energetic, not like a press release.
- English. Plain words; no buzzword stacks (the shared banned-phrase list applies).

## Rules

- Never invent clients, results, numbers, awards or testimonials. Name a client or a result only when the post's
  facts give it.
- A prototype, demo or concept piece is labelled as such ("Concept example"); never present it as a live client.
- No prices.
- The ask, when the post carries one, is to talk to Elettro (the brand site link from the facts).
